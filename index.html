ReturnFlow Privacy Policy

Track Returns. Recover Refunds.

Effective date: November 17, 2026

1. Introduction

ReturnFlow helps users organize purchases, returns, refund deadlines, reminders, and supporting evidence. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the ReturnFlow Android application.

2. Information stored on your device

You may enter or store purchase details, retailer information, prices, currencies, purchase dates, return deadlines, notes, reminders, return statuses, timeline events, receipt images, photographs, PDF attachments, and app preferences.

This information is stored locally in the app's private storage on your device. ReturnFlow does not require account registration and does not operate a developer-hosted database for your records.

3. How information is used

Locally stored information supports purchase organization, return and refund tracking, deadline reminders, purchase history, evidence storage, PDF report generation, and manual backup and restore.

4. Information sharing and third-party services

ReturnFlow does not include advertising or analytics services and does not send your purchase records, notes, receipts, or evidence to developer-operated servers.

Premium purchases, when available, are processed through Google Play Billing. The app receives purchase information, including purchase status and a purchase token, to manage premium access and restore purchases. Google Play processes payment credentials and payment transactions under Google's own terms and privacy practices.

5. Manual backups and file sharing

ReturnFlow allows you to export and import backups manually. A backup may contain purchase records, timelines, preferences, and evidence attachments.

You choose where an exported backup is saved using Android's document storage interface. If you choose a cloud-backed storage provider, that provider may process the file under its own privacy policy. You are responsible for protecting exported backup files.

ReturnFlow does not automatically upload your backups to a developer-operated cloud service.

6. Notifications

ReturnFlow can schedule local notifications for return deadlines and reminders. Android notification permission may be requested on supported versions. The app also uses system events to reschedule reminders when necessary, including after a device restart or time change.

7. Data security

ReturnFlow stores its records and evidence in Android's private application storage. Android automatic system backup is disabled in the app configuration. Manual backup files are outside the app's private storage, and their security depends on the destination you select.

8. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored information remains on your device until you delete it through available app controls or clear ReturnFlow's app storage. Uninstalling the app generally removes its locally stored app data.

Exported backup files are separate from the app. You must delete those files from their selected storage locations yourself.

9. Children's privacy

ReturnFlow is a general-purpose purchase and refund management tool and is not specifically directed at children.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this webpage.
